🌱 How to Play

🗑️
Sort the waste
Drag each item into the correct bin — recyclables, compost, landfill, or hazardous.
👆
Drag & drop
Grab items and drop them into the matching bin. On mobile, tap and hold then drag.
🌸
Blossom lifeline
Each mistake wilts a petal. Three petals lost and the round ends!
⭐
Earn points
Correct sorts add to your score. Combos and speed bonuses help you climb the leaderboard.
